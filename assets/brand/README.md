## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a completely new, exceptionally well-designed final app icon for FormatOwl, a modern file conversion and editing website. The previous icon felt amateurish and stiff. This must feel like a polished, memorable independent software brand designed by an expert identity designer.

One single icon only, square 1024x1024. A rich ultramarine-blue #263BC4 rounded-square tile nearly fills the canvas, with genuinely transparent outside corners. Center an ORIGINAL refined white owl symbol, approximately 72% of the tile width. Use a compact, beautifully balanced owl silhouette with gently pointed short ear tufts, expressive almond/circular facial countershapes, a very small beak, a rounded lower body, and one elegantly folded wing suggesting a folded sheet of paper. The paper idea should be subtle and integrated, never a separate document pictogram. Personality: intelligent, calm, friendly and capable. Smooth purposeful curves, optical balance, strong negative space, confident proportions. The two eyes should look attentive with modest pupils, not huge surprised cartoon eyes. White and one pale periwinkle accent maximum against solid ultramarine. Flat vector-like graphic with absolutely crisp edges, not a 3D render. Make it readable at 24px. Generous but not excessive inner clear space.

Art direction informed by research into geometric owl identity design: reduction, clear silhouette, refined curves and negative space. Invent an original design, not a copy of any known owl brand. Avoid shield-shaped face, heart-shaped mask, oversized diamond nose, layered clip-art circles, angry esports eyebrows, gradients, drop shadows, texture, glossy highlights, outlines too thin to survive at small size, intricate feathers, text, letters, wordmark, presentation board, mockup, extra icons or watermark. Deliver only the finished icon.

## Final cleanup prompt

Fix ONLY the rendering: make this a perfectly clean flat vector-like logo. The blue background MUST be a completely opaque, uniform solid #263BC4, covering the entire square canvas edge to edge, INCLUDING THE FOUR CORNERS. Output a full square blue image, NOT a transparent rounded rectangle; no alpha transparency anywhere in the output. The owl is completely opaque solid white with one solid pale periwinkle #A9B5FF wing accent. Eliminate all surface texture, grain, streaks, glows, highlights on the background, gradients, translucent cloudy patches, and distressed or uneven edges. Every interior shape must be uniformly flat filled, smooth and crisp. Keep this exact beautiful owl, do not redesign it. No text or mockup. One square image.

The final source is opaque. Web UI and the share card supply rounded corners; the Apple icon is masked by the OS. This avoids imperfect generated alpha on light backgrounds.
